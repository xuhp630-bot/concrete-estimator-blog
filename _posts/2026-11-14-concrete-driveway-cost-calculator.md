A new concrete driveway is one of the best investments you can make in your property. It's durable, low-maintenance, and adds curb appeal. But you need a solid budget before starting. Here's what concrete driveways cost in 2026.

## Average Costs (2026)

A standard concrete driveway runs **$4 to $15 per square foot** installed. For a typical 2-car driveway (approximately 600 sq ft):

| Level | Cost Range | What's Included |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| Basic | $2,400-$4,800 | Plain concrete, no reinforcement |
| Standard | $3,600-$7,200 | Rebar/wire mesh, standard finish |
| Premium | $6,000-$9,000 | Stamped/colored, drainage, sealing |

## What Changes the Price?

**Driveway size** is the primary cost driver. A single-lane driveway (10x20) costs much less than a double-wide (20x20 or larger).

**Thickness** — standard driveways use 4 inches. If you park heavy vehicles (RVs, trucks), go to 5-6 inches, which adds about 25% to material cost.

**Base preparation** — a properly installed gravel base (4-6 inches) prevents cracking and settling. This adds $1-3 per sq ft but saves thousands in repairs.

**Reinforcement** — wire mesh costs $0.30-0.50/sq ft, rebar costs $0.50-1.00/sq ft. Worth every penny for longevity.

## Cost-Saving Tips

1. **Bundle projects** — if you also need a walkway or patio, doing them together reduces mobilization costs
2. **Choose standard gray** — colored or stamped finishes add 30-50% to the price
3. **Off-season** — contractors may offer better rates in slower months
4. **Prepare the site yourself** — removing old concrete and prepping the base can save $500-1,500

## Long-Term Value

A well-built concrete driveway lasts 25-30 years with minimal maintenance. Compare that to asphalt (15-20 years, needs resealing every 3 years) and the lifetime cost often favors concrete.
