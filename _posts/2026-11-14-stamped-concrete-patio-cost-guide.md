A stamped concrete patio can transform a plain backyard into an elegant outdoor living space — but before you commit, you need a realistic budget. Here's what stamped concrete patios cost in 2026 and how to plan for it.

## Average Cost Ranges (2026)

Stamped concrete patio costs typically fall between **$8 to $18 per square foot**, depending on:

- **Pattern complexity** — simpler textures cost less, multi-pattern designs cost more
- **Color treatments** — integral color, release agents, and sealers
- **Site prep** — grading, demolition of existing surface, base material
- **Size** — larger patios often get a lower per-sq-ft rate
- **Location** — labor rates vary significantly by region

For a 200 sq ft patio, expect a total range of $1,600 to $3,600.

## What Drives Cost Up?

Several factors can push your project toward the higher end:

**Site preparation** is the biggest variable. If you need to remove an old deck, regrade the yard, or add a gravel base, expect $2-5 extra per square foot.

**Sealing and maintenance** — stamped concrete needs resealing every 2-3 years. Budget about $1-2 per sq ft for resealing.

**Color combinations** — a single color with a contrasting release agent is standard. Multi-color or hand-stained finishes add $3-6 per sq ft.

## Stamped vs. Other Patio Materials

| Material | Cost/sq ft | Durability | Maintenance |
|----------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Stamped concrete | $8-18 | High | Medium |
| Plain concrete | $4-8 | High | Low |
| Pavers | $10-25 | Medium | Medium |
| Natural stone | $15-30 | High | Low |
| Wood deck | $15-30 | Low | High |

Stamped concrete offers the best balance of aesthetics and cost for most homeowners.
