# Concrete Garage Floor Cost: What to Budget for a New or Resurfaced Floor

The garage floor takes more abuse than almost any surface in your home — vehicles, tools, chemical spills, and temperature extremes. Getting the right concrete floor in your garage isn't just aesthetic; it affects how long it lasts and how easy it is to maintain.

## Average Costs for Garage Floors in 2026

**New concrete garage floor:** $3-10 per sq ft installed  
**Resurfacing existing floor:** $2-5 per sq ft  
**Epoxy coating on existing concrete:** $3-7 per sq ft  

For a typical 2-car garage (480 sq ft), expect:
- New floor: $1,440 to $4,800
- Resurfacing: $960 to $2,400
- Epoxy coat: $1,440 to $3,360

## New Floor vs. Resurfacing: Which Is Right?

**Choose new concrete if:**
- Current floor has widespread structural cracks
- Floor has significant settlement or heaving
- Drainage needs to be redesigned
- Floor is 25+ years old with deterioration

**Choose resurfacing if:**
- Current floor is structurally sound
- Damage is primarily surface spalling or staining
- You want a fresh appearance without full demo cost

Resurfacing costs 40-60% less than a full replacement and can extend floor life by 10-15 years if done properly.

## Thickness Matters

Standard residential garage floors use **4-inch concrete**. If you:
- Park trucks, SUVs, or heavier vehicles → 5 inches
- Use the garage as a workshop with heavy equipment → 5-6 inches
- Plan vehicle lifts or heavier loads → 6 inches with extra rebar

Upgrading from 4" to 5" adds about 25% to your concrete cost.

| Reinforcement | Cost Added | Best For |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| Wire mesh | $0.30-0.50/sq ft | Light residential use |
| Rebar | $0.50-1.00/sq ft | Heavy vehicles, workshops |
| Fiber reinforcement | $0.20-0.40/sq ft | Crack control |
| None | $0 | Temporary/light use only |

## Floor Finish Options

The finish affects both aesthetics and durability:

**Broom finish (standard)** — $0 extra. Rough texture for traction. Very durable.  
**Smooth trowel** — $0.50-1.00/sq ft extra. Easier to clean, looks more finished.  
**Exposed aggregate** — $1-2/sq ft extra. Attractive look, good traction.  
**Epoxy coating** — $3-7/sq ft for professional application. Protects concrete, easier to clean, resistant to chemical stains.

## Tips to Keep Costs Down

1. **Pour during mild weather** — extreme heat or cold affects curing and may require special additives
2. **Prep the site yourself** — cleaning, minor crack repair, and clearing the garage can reduce labor cost
3. **Bundle with driveway work** — same contractor, reduced mobilization cost
4. **Consider DIY resurfacing** — small garages are manageable; rental equipment is affordable
